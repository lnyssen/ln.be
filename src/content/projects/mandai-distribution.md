Complete redesign and brand refresh for an independent music distributor
and mail order platform established in Brussels in 2002. Specializing in
experimental, noise, jazz, and underground vinyl releases, the new
identity balances raw industrial aesthetics with high-efficiency
e-commerce UX. The system streamlines catalog navigation across 2,500+
physical references while honoring two decades of underground music
culture.
