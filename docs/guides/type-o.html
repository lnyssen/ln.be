00Start

Take a real typeface. Break it on purpose.

Type-O starts from a real variable font and runs its outlines through a stack of operators: it cuts, slides, inflates, melts or redraws every letter. What comes out is not an image. It is a working font, ready to install, that you can type with.

Your first font in four steps

  1. Pick a sourceUnder 01 Source, press Change and browse 36 families, sorted by kind: grotesk, serif, didone, slab, script and more. Its axes, such as weight or optical size, appear as sliders under the name.
  2. Build a stackUnder 02 Stack, add operators with the + buttons. They apply from top to bottom, each one working on the result of the one above it. Open an operator to tune its settings.
  3. Or let chance start itUnder 03 Random, Roll a typeface deals a whole stack at once. Keep what you like, then refine it by hand.
  4. Export itOpen the Export tab, name your font, choose a format and press Generate & download.

Managing the stack

◉ MuteSwitches an operator off without losing its settings. A quick way to see what it really does.
↑ ↓ MoveChanges the order. Order matters: an outline followed by a halftone is not a halftone followed by an outline.
✕ RemoveTakes the operator out of the stack.
Same twiceAny operator can be added more than once, with different settings each time.
Three ways to lookWord shows a sample word, large. Alphabet lays out every glyph in a grid, the best view to judge a whole font. Source shows the starting typeface, so you can compare.

02Chance & export

From a roll of the dice to a real font file.

Random

Roll a typeface picks a stack and its settings from a seed, a simple number. The same seed always gives the same result, so a typeface you like can be found again. Next moves to the following seed. The legibility score beside it gives a rough idea of how readable the letters remain.

Export

01 NameThe family name your font will carry once installed.
02 FormatTTF or OTF to install on macOS or Windows, for Adobe apps and Figma. WOFF and WOFF2 for the web.
03 BuildGenerate & download builds the file. Test it here tries it right in the page.
04 AlsoA specimen sheet in SVG, fully vector, and an animation in WebM that plays the stack from nothing to its full setting.

Save & reopen

Save (⌘S) writes a .typeo project file: the source, the stack, every setting and the seed. Open (⌘O) brings it back, so the exact same font can be rebuilt later.

The license travels with the fontEvery export is a modified version of an open-source family and stays under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license and the original copyright are embedded in the file. You can use it anywhere, including print, web, apps, logos and commercial work. You can share it and bundle it with software.
