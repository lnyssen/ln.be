Player's guide

COOKIGOTCHI

A pocket frog that lives on your phone. Hatch it, name it, feed it, play with it — and watch it grow, day after day.

Laurent Nyssen · Experiment
Get started

An egg on a lily pad

Cookigotchi is a virtual pet in the spirit of the nineties classics, rebuilt as a web app. It lives in real time: your frog gets hungry, sleepy and lonely even while the phone is in your pocket.

A note on labelsThe game speaks French. Its words are kept here exactly as they appear on screen, with a translation alongside.

Put it on your phone

  1. Open the democookigotchi-demo.vercel.app, in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone.
  2. Add it to the home screenChrome: the three dots, then Ajouter à l'écran d'accueil (add to home screen). Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Launch it from the iconIt opens full-screen, like an app, and keeps working without a connection.

The first minutes

  1. Say who you areThe game asks your first name — it will greet you by it.
  2. Tap the eggThe more you tap, the sooner it hatches.
  3. Name your tadpoleType a name, or tap Autre idée (another idea) for a suggestion.

Take care of it

Four gauges, six buttons

At the top of the screen, four gauges tell you how your frog feels. The fuller they are, the happier it is. When it needs something, a bubble appears above its head with the matching icon.

Amour · loveIt wants cuddles.
Faim · hungerIts tummy rumbles.
Énergie · energyFull of beans, or worn out.
Propreté · cleanSpotless — or time for a bath.

The buttons along the bottom

Câlin · cuddleFills the love gauge. You can also tap the frog itself.
Miam · yumA cookie. If it isn't hungry, it says no.
Jeu · playPick a mini-game. Playing makes it happy… and a little tired.
Dodo · napA nap when it's sleepy. Tap again to wake it.
Bain · bathSplash — clean again.
Nounou · babysitterPauses everything while you're away: off to school or to a friend's, time stops at the pond. Tap any button when you're back.
Cookigotchi — Player's guide02 · Care
Its days and yours

It lives on your clock

NightFrom 8 pm it falls asleep under the stars, and wakes at 7 am. At night its gauges drop much more slowly.
SchoolOn school days the pond pauses itself during class hours. No school today? Tap Ombeline and choose Pas d'école aujourd'hui. For the holidays, turn off École auto in the settings (the gear).
WeatherThe sky over the pond follows the real weather: sun, clouds, rain, snow, fog.

It grows up

For real, day after day. The little green bar at the top shows when it will change.

Babyfirst 6 hours
Childuntil day 4
Teendays 5 to 10
Adultfrom day 11
Love and familyOnce it's an adult and happy, another frog comes to visit one day, a heart above its head. Tap it: love at first sight. Later an egg appears on the pond. Tap it when you're ready — the parents move to the Grande Mare (the big pond), and you raise their baby, a mix of both their colours. The button with two hearts opens the family album.
The golden frogTake very good care of an adult frog for two weeks and it may turn… golden, with a tiny crown. Shh, it's a secret.

Play and collect

Games, coins and Ombeline

Three mini-games

Gobe-mouches · fly-catcherTap an insect and the frog flicks its tongue. Fly +1, firefly +3, dragonfly +5; chain catches for combos ×2, ×3, ×4. Watch out for the wasps: −3 and a dizzy head.
Chanson des bulles · bubble songBubbles light up to music. Remember the order and play it back; every round adds a note.
Cache-cache · hide and seekA tadpole hides under a leaf, then the leaves shuffle. Follow it with your eyes.

Every game keeps your best score.

Coins, shop and spellbook

Coins come from the games, the daily quests, the achievements — and from simply visiting your frog every day. Tap your coins to open Ombeline's shop: hats for the frog, decorations for the pond. The purple book next to it is your grimoire: quests, achievements and family.

OmbelineOn the bank sits Ombeline, keeper of the pond. She never smiles. Well — almost never. With every cuddle, cookie and game, your frog smiles a little more, and so does she. The pond warms up, the roses blush, the sun breaks through… and go all the way, and a rainbow may appear.
A secret code to tryGear, then Code secret, then type DEMO: a small treasure to try out the shop.
And if I forget?Don't panic: your frog never dies. Forgotten for a while, it sulks or turns dry and grey. A few cuddles, a bath and a cookie, and it's itself again.
